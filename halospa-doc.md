## 3. Services and Products Offered

Halo Spa specializes in a fusion of traditional scalp treatments and modern facial technology.

### Scalp Spa Services

*The most popular treatment is the Signature Scalp Spa.*

- **Signature Scalp Spa:** $108 (1 hour) – The foundation of scalp health. This treatment begins with a thorough scalp analysis to identify specific concerns such as dryness, oiliness, or sensitivity. Includes deep cleansing with specialized products to remove buildup, exfoliation to promote circulation, a nourishing scalp treatment mask, and a relaxing scalp massage to stimulate hair follicles and promote healthy hair growth. Perfect for first-time guests or those seeking regular scalp maintenance.

- **Deluxe Scalp Spa:** $168 (1 hour 30 min) – The ultimate scalp indulgence. This comprehensive treatment incorporates everything from the Luxe service plus additional relaxation and rejuvenation steps including hot towel therapy, specialized steaming to open pores and enhance product absorption, extended massage time with focus on pressure points, and customized treatment serums tailored to your specific scalp concerns. Ideal for those experiencing scalp issues or seeking maximum relaxation.

### Facial Treatments

- **Mini Facial:** $48 (30 min) – A quick yet effective refresh perfect for busy schedules or maintenance between full facials. Includes gentle cleansing to remove impurities, light exfoliation, toning to balance pH, and a hydrating moisturizer with SPF. Provides an instant glow and refreshed appearance without the time commitment of a full treatment.

- **Signature Facial:** $108 (1 hour) – A classic European-style facial customized to your skin type. Includes thorough double cleansing, professional exfoliation to remove dead skin cells, steam treatment to open pores, detailed extractions when needed, a relaxing facial massage to boost circulation, a customized mask based on your skin's needs, and finishing with serums, moisturizer, and eye cream. Suitable for all skin types and addresses multiple concerns including hydration, texture, and tone.

*Note: Gift certificates are available for most services at the prices listed above.*

## 4. Business Mission and Details

- **Mission:** At Halo Spa, the mission is to offer tailored treatments crafted specifically for each individual. There is a heavy emphasis on "nurturing the scalp" and "enhancing the health and vitality of the hair" as the foundation for overall beauty.

- **Philosophy:** The spa focuses on a "head-to-toe" renewal philosophy. They believe that scalp health is often overlooked in traditional beauty routines and aim to provide a relaxing environment (described by clients as clean, soothing, and intentional) where guests can decompress while receiving results-driven treatments.

- **History:** Located in the heart of Orange County, the spa has established itself as a local destination for "Head Spa" enthusiasts—a trend popularized by Japanese and Korean beauty standards that prioritizes scalp detoxification to promote healthy hair growth and mental relaxation.
